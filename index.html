§01 — Introduction

About me

A detail-oriented Business Analyst who sits between the people with the problem and the people building the solution. My job is to make sure both are describing the same thing.

I lead requirement-gathering sessions, prepare BRDs, FRDs, use cases and user stories, and turn them into process flows and wireframes in Figma and Visio. I facilitate Agile ceremonies, manage sprint lifecycles and run UAT through to sign-off.

I also manage end-to-end government tender bids on the GeM portal, from RFP analysis and B2G bid structuring to compliance documentation. Before that, I researched AI and LLM solutions at Nebula9.ai, and as a B.Tech CSE student I built apps for PGIMER Chandigarh and Gurugram Police.
